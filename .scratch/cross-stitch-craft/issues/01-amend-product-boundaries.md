## Notes

This ticket is governance, not code. It exists first because `PRODUCT.md` currently forbids the feature the other four tickets build.

`CONTEXT.md` adds Stitcher, Craft, and Chart Cell; generalizes Colorwork Chart, Pattern Project, Yarn Inventory, and Color Match to cover both crafts; and adds an Undecided section holding three naming questions (Yarn Inventory, the cross-stitch counterpart of Knit-ready Pattern, and whether an umbrella noun for Knitter and Stitcher should exist).

Because there is no approved umbrella noun and `CONTEXT.md` lists _maker_ under Avoid, the spec's user stories 6–10 and tickets 02–04 were reworded from "As a Maker" to "As a Knitter or Stitcher".
