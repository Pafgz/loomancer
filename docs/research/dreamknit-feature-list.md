# Dreamknit feature list

Source: provided by the product owner on 2026-07-24.  
Purpose: reference backlog for later parity comparisons. Not an MVP checklist.

## Design and customization

- **AI Pattern Generator** — Convert text descriptions into stitch patterns.
- **Colorway Visualizer** — Preview yarn combinations on 3D models.
- **Stitch Library** — Access thousands of cable, lace, and colorwork charts.
- **Custom Sizing Engine** — Input body measurements for tailored instructions.
- **Import Tool** — Convert PDFs and images into editable digital charts.

## Project management

- **Smart Row Counter** — Track progress via voice commands or screen taps.
- **Multi-Project Dashboard** — Monitor status, deadlines, and yarn allocations.
- **Stash Integration** — Link specific stash yarn to active projects.
- **Time Estimator** — Predict completion dates based on knitting speed.
- **Interactive Checklists** — Check off completed pattern sections automatically.

## Inventory and stash tracking

- **Barcode Scanner** — Scan yarn labels to log brand, weight, and dye lot.
- **Needle and Notion Inventory** — Track sizes, cable lengths, and materials owned.
- **Visual Palette Organizer** — Group yarn inventory by color family.
- **Shopping List Alerts** — Notify missing materials needed for upcoming projects.
- **Substitution Suggestion** — Recommend stash alternatives based on pattern gauge.

## Community and sharing

- **Live Knit-Alongs** — Join virtual rooms with video and chat features.
- **Pattern Marketplace** — Buy, sell, or share user-generated designs.
- **Social Feed** — Post project updates, photos, and modifications.
- **Group Challenges** — Participate in community-wide speed or charity drives.
- **Tester Matching** — Connect designers with pattern testers directly.

## Advanced accessibility

- **Voice-Guided Mode** — Dictate pattern steps hands-free while knitting.
- **High-Contrast View** — Adjust visibility settings for low-light crafting.
- **Left-Handed Mirroring** — Flip charts and video tutorials instantly.
- **Offline Access** — View patterns and counters without internet.
